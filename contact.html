Frequently Asked Questions

Essential details on tender response times, QuickPay settlements, insurance qualifications, and detention policies.

On average, loads tendered on FreightHawk receive initial carrier matches in under 4.2 minutes. Our automated pairing engine scans 12,500+ pre-vetted carriers based on equipment type, active ELD location, hours-of-service (HOS) availability, and preferred backhaul corridors.
Motor carriers who upload clean electronic Proof of Delivery (e-POD) photos along with the signed Bill of Lading through the mobile portal receive direct ACH or wire transfer authorization within 120 minutes. No factoring company notices of assignment (NOA) or hidden check fees are deducted.
All motor carriers must possess active FMCSA interstate operating authority with at least 90 days tenure, a satisfactory or non-rated safety record, minimum $100,000 active cargo insurance, and $1,000,000 public auto liability coverage. Our compliance bots continuously query FMCSA registries every 24 hours.
FreightHawk enforces an automatic $75/hour detention reimbursement after 2 free hours of wait time. Facility arrival and departure are recorded via driver smartphone GPS geofencing (0.5-mile boundary), eliminating dock disputes and manual check-in paperwork.
Yes. FreightHawk provides modern RESTful APIs and supports standard EDI transactions (EDI 204 Load Tender, EDI 990 Response, EDI 214 Shipment Status, and EDI 210 Invoice). High-volume shippers can tender hundreds of loads daily with automated rate confirmation.
Our in-house claims division operates 24/7. In the rare event of cargo damage, overages, or shortages, dedicated adjusters investigate using digital timestamped photos, temperature logs, and GPS records to adjudicate claims under standard Carmack Amendment rules within 72 hours.
