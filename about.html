Why We Built FreightHawk

The multi-billion dollar freight industry has long suffered from predatory middlemen. We replaced opaque phone-brokerage with algorithmic precision and verified trust.

The Legacy Brokerage Model

Opaque • Adversarial • Inefficient

Traditional freight brokers operate as black-box intermediaries, extracting hidden margins from both shippers and carriers while slowing down essential cash flow.

  • Concealed Rate Margins: Brokers pocket 20% to 35% of the linehaul fee without disclosing it to either party.
  • 30-to-60 Day Net Terms: Motor carriers are forced to use high-interest invoice factoring companies just to fund diesel fuel.
  • Phantom Load Postings: Load boards cluttered with stale, fake, or pre-booked freight designed solely to harvest carrier phone calls.
  • Manual Check-Call Harassment: Drivers interrupted multiple times per day with phone calls rather than modern GPS geofencing.

The FreightHawk Autonomous Model

100% Transparent • Instant • Direct

A direct digital platform connecting verified shippers with compliant motor carriers. Every linehaul rate is transparently visible, contracts are signed instantly, and funds settle in 2 hours.

  • Guaranteed Rate Transparency: 100% visibility into linehaul payouts and fuel indices. Zero hidden markups or deductions.
  • Under 2-Hour QuickPay Settlements: Direct ACH deposit or fleet fuel card funding immediately upon electronic POD upload.
  • Verified Live Inventory Only: Every load on our board is backed by an active shipper tender and legally binding RateCon.
  • Silent ELD Telematics Checkpoints: Continuous GPS telemetry and geofence triggers automatically log arrivals, departures, and detention.
